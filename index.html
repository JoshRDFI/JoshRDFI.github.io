Featured · RDF Industries

QOR

Governed multi-agent AI for enterprise and regulated work

QOR runs in your own cloud or on-premises, so your data stays inside your control boundary. Its agents work under scoped authority, wait for human approval where it matters, and leave a full audit trail. I architected it and built it.

Learn about QOR Ask about licensing
  • Governed multi-agent runtime. Strategic, tactical and specialist agents coordinate work with controlled access to tools and data.
  • Scoped authority and human approvals. Each agent acts only within the authority it is granted, sensitive steps wait for a person, and access can be revoked mid-execution.
  • Full audit trail. Actions, approvals and refusals are recorded, with provenance back to the source.
  • Deploys where you need it. Single-tenant in your cloud or on-premises, with local model serving. Fully air-gapped environments are supported.
  • Compliance-aligned design. Maps to NIST 800-171 / CMMC Level 2, SOC 2, HIPAA and ITAR/DFARS requirements.
